# Matriz — Constraints and Assumptions (v0.2)

**Status:** Draft; no proposed rule, priority, or delivery commitment is client-approved. The only established needs are organizing projects (F01), managing tasks (F02), and tracking progress (F03). See [requirements](requirements.md) for the proposed behaviour and [user stories](user-stories.md) for acceptance scenarios. Revised on 2026-10-29 with the project team's provisional decisions ([ADR-002](adr/ADR-002-phase-scope-and-implementation-authorization.md)); team decisions are recorded as assumptions and interim answers, never as confirmed requirements.

## Security and privacy

- **Authentication:** [Assumption A-014](#assumptions) proposes identity verification before access. The client has not decided the identity method, access provisioning, who may create projects, or the session policy (OQ-001, OQ-018, OQ-027). The team provisionally uses sign-in with prepared accounts ([Assumption A-016](#assumptions)) and signed access tokens ([ADR-005](adr/ADR-005-authentication-with-jwt.md)). See [NFR-001](requirements.md#non-functional-requirements-and-unresolved-quality-criteria).
- **Authorization:** [Assumption A-001](#assumptions) and [Assumption A-002](#assumptions) propose enforcing per-project membership and role permissions for both listings and direct attempts to view or change a project or task (OQ-019). Refused changes leave the information unchanged. The team provisionally makes a single API the only entry point, checked on every request ([ADR-004](adr/ADR-004-rest-api-single-entry-point.md)). See [NFR-002](requirements.md#non-functional-requirements-and-unresolved-quality-criteria), the [permission matrix](requirements.md#permission-matrix-proposed), and the [exceptional cases](user-stories.md#edge-cases-and-exceptions).
- **Data protection and GDPR:** categories of personal data, access to them, retention, and applicable legal and internal obligations must be identified with the client and competent stakeholders before concrete controls or periods are specified (OQ-011). No claim of GDPR compliance is made; see [NFR-003](requirements.md#non-functional-requirements-and-unresolved-quality-criteria). The team's interim decisions add personal data that this work must cover: login names, display names, and password hashes ([Assumption A-017](#assumptions)), member lists visible to all members ([Assumption A-027](#assumptions)), free-text comments with their authors ([Assumption A-023](#assumptions)), deleted items kept with no defined retention period ([Assumption A-022](#assumptions)), and, as an unconfirmed proposal, the display names of everyone known to Matriz shown to any Project Owner choosing whom to add ([Assumption A-030](#assumptions)). Until OQ-011 is answered, only fictitious data is used ([Assumption A-016](#assumptions)).

Technologies and security mechanisms were chosen provisionally by the project team on 2026-10-29 ([ADR-003](adr/ADR-003-technology-stack.md), [ADR-005](adr/ADR-005-authentication-with-jwt.md)). The architectural style is recorded in the [architecture definition](architecture.md) and [ADR-001](adr/ADR-001-modular-monolith.md).
